# Treatment damage and post-flush recovery in coco

## Separate treatment damage from pest damage

- Read whole-canopy drooping that starts 1–3 days after a spray, with yellow lower leaves and brown crisp tips, as treatment damage — not a deficiency and not a new pest.
- Read silvery suction spots, black frass specks, or live insects on the leaf underside as pest damage instead.
- Apply a foliar product once, then wait 5–7 days before repeating; consecutive daily neem applications on seedlings produce exactly the drooping and margin burn above.
- Do not combine a soil drench and a foliar spray of the same product on the same day.

## Flush only after the treatment has stopped

- Stop every spray and drench first; a flush does not remove product already taken up by the plant.
- Flush at pH 6,0–6,2 in several passes and read drain EC after each pass; stop when drain EC is within about 100 µS/cm of the input EC.
- Empty the tray after every pass, because standing runoff re-accumulates the salts just washed out.

## Refill the substrate after a flush

- Treat coco as nutrient-free: after a flush, plain pH-corrected water deepens the yellowing on lower leaves.
- Feed on the first watering after the flush, never water alone.
- Compute the dose from the base EC of the water. Maintal tap runs about 0300 µS/cm; Hakaphos Blau at 0,5 g/L adds about 0790 µS/cm, so about 0,25 g/L (1,25 g per 5 L) lands near 0700 µS/cm total.
- Mix, then read the meter; the calculation sets the starting point and the measured value decides.
- Hold pH at 6,0 and feed from the bottom; do not push the solution through as a flush.

## Rehydrate hydrophobic coco from below

- Treat coco that pulls away from the pot wall and channels water straight down as hydrophobic; a fast top watering bypasses the root zone.
- Set the pots in the tray with about 1 L of solution for 20–30 minutes in two passes, then discard what is left.
- Water again only once the pot feels clearly light.

## Judge recovery on new growth

- Ignore old yellow or brown leaves; they stay damaged and do not justify a feed change.
- Check the centre and newest tips after 3–5 days: upright and green confirms the course, continued collapse of new growth means re-diagnose.
